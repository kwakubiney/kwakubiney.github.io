This is more of a rant. 

I feel a lot of things at the moment. I hate attachment. The ones where it feels like you have known them forever, but met by accident last week. I get lost in her eyes every time I look at her, her smile is the prettiest I have ever seen. My brain is screaming at me, telling me to rethink this whole thing. Is she a good person? Does she know what she wants? Can I take this seriously? What if she is lowkey unreasonable? A little bit too egoistic? What if she just likes the idea of being chased? Her smile and kisses say otherwise. She wants to be with me, but another part of me doubts it. Should I just go ahead with it? It does not have to be like this, if it will end terribly.
I love being her friend, above everything, but now I have feelings for her. Feelings feel really great with her(?). Her legs did the happy dance when I got her the necklace. I felt so much joy. I really like her. I just hate doubt. I am not very good at being in the middle, and there have been way too many highs and lows this past week, I cannot take it. We went out with the kids today. They are so lovely. It felt like something I would want to do with her, with our own children. Our little stares at each other feel longer than they are. I could look at her all day. 
I think she feels the same way about me, and probably also thinks I might be manipulating her or whatever. I am thinking about her right now. I can tell she is upset, but I cannot make her feel better. I just texted her again when I said I was not going to, for the second time. I am such a beg. But I miss her (was with her for 50% of the past 48 hours). It's the weekend, I want every minute available to have an 'us' moment in it. Weekdays are not the same, although I try to make them be, for us. I love her, or whatever, I am just going to post this. She wants to talk, I feel better now.
